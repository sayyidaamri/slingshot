Privacy policy

We're not interested in hoarding your data: we only collect and store the minimum when you visit Slingshot.

About

At Slingshot we are committed to respecting and protecting your personal data. This policy describes how we collect and handle your personal data to make sure we're in accordance with our legal obligations. If you are reading this notice it is because you may have visited our site, followed us on social media or contacted us directly.

Social media and third party websites

When you visit our pages on social platforms, the privacy practices of those platforms apply. We encourage you to read their notices. Our site may link to third-party websites; if you follow these links you will leave our website and we cannot accept liability for how those sites use your data.

Information we collect

In running and maintaining our site we may collect and process information about your use of the site, including pages viewed and resources accessed, traffic data and location data. Where you have signed up to our newsletter we also store your email address. You may unsubscribe at any time.

Sharing of data

We only share personal data when we have a legally permitted reason to do so, and only with providers of software, hosting and professional services who protect it to the same standard we do.

How long do we keep it?

We keep your data only for as long as is necessary to satisfy the purpose for which it was provided, and for a reasonable period after your last interaction with us.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to its processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@slingshot.example.

Updates

We make changes to this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
