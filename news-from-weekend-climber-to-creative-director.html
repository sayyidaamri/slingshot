From Weekend Climber to Creative Director

At our Lisbon studio we brought together founders, athletes and marketers for an early-morning session on a simple question – and why the safest answer is often the riskiest move a brand can make.

Are you a morning runner or an evening runner? According to our founder, there is no middle ground – and that is exactly the point. Brands that try to be for everyone end up meaning nothing to anyone.

As surprising as it sounds, that one question unlocked the whole conversation. The room split instantly, opinions flew, and within minutes we were talking about conviction, identity and the courage to choose.

Conviction beats consensus

The brands that grow fastest in sport are rarely the most polished. They are the ones that pick a side – a community, a ritual, a feeling – and refuse to water it down for the sake of reach.

We see it on the shelf, on the start line and in the feed: people choose brands that choose them back. Momentum comes from specificity, not from averages.

“Nobody falls in love with a brand that is trying not to offend them.”

Our advice is simple. Find the people who already care, design for them first, and let the rest of the market catch up. It is less comfortable – and far more effective.
